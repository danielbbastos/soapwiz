Honey Oat Swirl Bars

This cheerful bar is a great project for a rainy weekend. The honey oatmeal fragrance smells like a warm breakfast, and the swirl is easier than it looks. If you're new to cold process, read our lye safety post first!

What You'll Need:
5-Pound Wood Mold
13.6 oz. Coconut Oil
13.6 oz. Olive Oil
9.5 oz. Palm Oil
2.7 oz. Castor Oil
1.4 oz. Sweet Almond Oil
5.6 oz. Sodium Hydroxide Lye
13.2 oz. Distilled Water
2.5 oz. Honey Oatmeal Fragrance Oil
1 tsp. Colloidal Oatmeal
1 Tbsp. Sodium Lactate
Titanium Dioxide

ONE: Slowly and carefully add 5.6 oz. of lye to 13.2 oz. of distilled water and stir until clear. Set aside to cool.

TWO: Melt and combine the coconut, olive, palm, castor and sweet almond oils. Once the lye water and oils are around 120°F, add the sodium lactate to the cooled lye water.

THREE: Disperse 1 teaspoon of titanium dioxide into 1 tablespoon of sweet almond oil, and mix the colloidal oatmeal into the fragrance oil.

FOUR: Pour the lye water into the oils and stick blend to thin trace. Add the fragrance, split the batter and swirl. Cure for 4–6 weeks.
