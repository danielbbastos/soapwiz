A batch made with full water and a 5% SF comes out like this for me:

Olive 30 oz (57.36%)
Apricot 8 oz (15.29%)
Coconut 6.3 oz (12.04%)
Castor 3.7 oz (7.07%)
Shea 4.3 oz (8.22%)

Water 19.874 ounces / 563.4 grams
Lye 6.987 ounces / 198.07 grams

You wouldn't add your almond milk on top of the water, you'd use it as part of your water, so you'd use 8.5 oz of almond milk and 11.3oz of water.
